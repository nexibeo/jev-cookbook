# Roadmap

New recipes arrive every Monday until mid-October 2026, three at a time. Each one ships with a runnable script, a small hand-labelled dataset and results from a live run, like recipes 01 to 14.

## Week of September 21, 2026

- [x] **15 · Gmail labeler:** connect your Gmail and label incoming email (newsletter, receipt, personal, work, calendar, suspicious…) with "needs reply" and "deadline". Published early, on September 19.
- [ ] **16 · CV screening against a job description:** composite scores per requirement, hard requirements checked in code, and an explanation per candidate.
- [ ] **17 · Review analysis by aspect:** for each product review, which aspects are mentioned (price, quality, delivery, support…) and the sentiment for each.

## Week of September 28, 2026

- [ ] **18 · Contract clause review:** clause types plus risk flags (auto-renewal, unlimited liability, one-sided termination), routed to a lawyer by severity.
- [ ] **19 · Citation and RAG passage checks:** does a retrieved passage support the claim, contradict it, or carry an injected instruction?
- [ ] **20 · Chatbot intent routing and tool choice:** pick the right tool or function and its closed-set arguments, or hand off to a person.

## Week of October 5, 2026

- [ ] **21 · Data quality checks:** find rows whose description contradicts their category, test or junk rows, and placeholder values.
- [ ] **22 · Product attributes by picking:** colour, size and material from messy product titles, chosen from candidate lists.
- [ ] **23 · Action items from meeting notes:** which lines are action items, who owns each (from the attendee list), and the due date.

## Week of October 12, 2026

- [ ] **24 · Matching people to options:** match buyers to property listings (or candidates to jobs) on stated preferences, with explainable scores.
- [ ] **25 · Web page audit:** page type, search intent, thin or duplicate content, and a missing call to action, for an SEO review.
- [ ] **26 · Customer feedback to tickets:** turn raw feedback into deduplicated bug reports and feature requests with product area and priority.
