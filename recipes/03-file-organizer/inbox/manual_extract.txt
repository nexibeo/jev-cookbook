QUICK START - AeroBrew 300 Espresso Machine
1. Fill the water tank to the MAX line with fresh cold water.
2. Press the power button; the light blinks while heating and stays on when ready (about 30 seconds).
3. Insert the portafilter and press the single or double cup button.
Descaling: every 3 months, run the descaling programme by holding both cup buttons for 5 seconds.
Warranty: 2 years from date of purchase.
