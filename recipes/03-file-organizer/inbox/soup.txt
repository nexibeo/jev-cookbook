Weeknight red lentil soup (4 bowls)
1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 garlic cloves, 200 g red lentils, 1 tin tomatoes, 1 L stock, 1 tsp cumin.
Soften the onion and carrot for 8 minutes, add garlic and cumin, then lentils, tomatoes and stock.
Simmer 20 minutes and blend half. Finish with lemon juice and yoghurt.
