Grandma's lemon drizzle cake
Serves 8. Prep 15 min, bake 45 min at 180 C.
225g butter, 225g caster sugar, 4 eggs, 225g self-raising flour, zest of 2 lemons.
Beat butter and sugar, add eggs one at a time, fold in flour and zest.
Bake in a loaf tin. While warm, pour over the juice of 1 lemon mixed with 85g icing sugar.
